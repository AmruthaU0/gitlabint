Machine learning is a subfield of artificial intelligence (AI) that enables computers to learn from data and make decisions or predictions without being explicitly programmed for specific tasks. It works by identifying patterns in large datasets and using these patterns to make inferences about new, unseen data. There are several types of machine learning, including supervised learning, where the model is trained on labeled data; unsupervised learning, which finds hidden patterns in unlabeled data; and reinforcement learning, where an agent learns by interacting with an environment and receiving feedback in the form of rewards or penalties. Machine learning is widely used across various industries—for example, in healthcare for disease prediction, in finance for fraud detection, and in retail for personalized recommendations. As data becomes more abundant and computing power grows, machine learning continues to drive innovation and improve decision-making in countless applications.
